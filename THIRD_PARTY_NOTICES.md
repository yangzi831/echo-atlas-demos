# Third-Party Notices

## Natural Earth

The Global Earth land mask uses public-domain Natural Earth 1:50m and 1:110m
land polygon data.

- Source: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/
- Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

## NASA Visible Earth

The restrained surface and cloud layers use resized NASA Visible Earth imagery.

- Blue Marble surface: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-map/
- Cloud layer: https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/
